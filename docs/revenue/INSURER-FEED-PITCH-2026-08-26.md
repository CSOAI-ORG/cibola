# INSURER-FEED PITCH PACK — signed independent measurement feed (Black Swan move 8) · 2026-08-26

**Thesis:** AI-liability insurers need an independent measurement input that is NOT the party
underwriting the risk (AIUC's standard-writing + accrediting + underwriting model drew the
issuer-pays critique; Moody's/S&P paid $1.5bn for issuer-pays). CSOAI slots in as the neutral
evidence layer: **a feed you can price against, from a body that will never underwrite and
therefore never conflicts.**

## The product (one paragraph)
A recurring subscription feed of Ed25519-signed, stranger-verifiable governance measurements for
underwritten AI systems: 22-axis board scores (15 measured / 7 honest UNMEASURED), per-card
receipts (RFC 9943 SCITT + RFC 3161 + OpenTimestamps), append-only corrections ledger, and the
three-state verdict (measured / unmeasured / refused) — deterministic, no-LLM-judge, publishable.
Free public verification; metered underwriting-grade feed.
