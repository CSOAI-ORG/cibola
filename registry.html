Measurement Registry of Record

The registry of record is the measurement board — an append-only, content-addressed, hash-chained measurement register. Each row is a signed measurement credential on the RFC 9943 / RFC 9942 substrate. A credential is evidence of what was measured and when. It is not a certification, endorsement, or conformity mark, and must not be presented as one.

did:web:csoai.org resolves to the published Ed25519 display in https://councilof.ai/.well-known/did.json (also served at https://csoai.org/.well-known/did.json). A stranger verifies a card offline with the published key listed there.

Verify a card

Run the human one-command check against a card, its receipt and its RFC 3161 anchor, or use the offline verify-kit bundle:

dorado verify-all --card CARD.json --receipt RECEIPT.json --anchor ANCHOR.json
# optional identity pinning to the published key:
  --pubkey <did:web:csoai.org#card-attestation-1 published key>

See the verify page and the stranger-verify walkthrough.

Doctrine

  • Measurement, never certification. A credential is evidence of what was measured and when; never accreditation, endorsement, or conformity.
  • Neutrality. The scored entity never pays for its measurement. A vendor can buy the data; a vendor can never buy the score.
  • Join on weights, not names. Two cards that name the same model but measure different weights are distinct.
