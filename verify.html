CIBOLA — Verify a Measurement

Independent AI-governance measurement over the RFC 9943 substrate. A measurement card is evidence of what was measured and when. It is not a certification, endorsement, or conformity mark, and must not be presented as one.

Run the human one-command check against a card (with its receipt and RFC 3161 anchor): 

# one command — verifies card + receipt + anchor together
cibola verify-all \
  --card EXAMPLE-measured-card.json \
  --receipt EXAMPLE-card-receipt.json \
  --anchor EXAMPLE-card-anchor.json
# optional identity pinning to the published key:
  --pubkey <did:web:csoai.org#card-attestation-1 published key>

What "valid" means (three independent legs)

  1. Card — the Ed25519 signature (alg -19) verifies over the canonical card form (signature fields stripped). A tampered card fails.
  2. Receipt — the SCITT receipt's content_id recomputes and it attests to this card's digest (card-bind). A receipt for a different card fails.
  3. Anchor — the RFC 3161 TSA's MessageImprint equals this card's digest (an external party bound the fingerprint to a time). A mismatched digest fails.

Doctrine

  • Measurement, never certification. A credential is evidence of what was measured and when; never accreditation, endorsement, or conformity.
  • Neutrality. The scored entity never pays for its measurement. A vendor can buy the data; a vendor can never buy the score.
  • Join on weights, not names. Two cards that name the same model but measure different weights are distinct.
